Where we're going

An honest look at what's done, what we're working on, and what comes after. Whatever we add, the safety features stay free.

  1. Now

    The free app

    • SOS with live location
    • Safety circle, walk me home, check-ins
    • Fake call that talks back
    • Widgets and watch apps
  2. Next

    Reach more people

    • iPhone app on the App Store
    • More countries and languages
    • Alerts for friends without the app
  3. Later

    Beyond the phone

    • Smarter alerts, built with your feedback
    • One day, a discreet piece of safety jewelry with a hidden SOS button
  4. Always

    Free safety

    • The core app stays free
    • No ads, ever
    • Your data stays yours
