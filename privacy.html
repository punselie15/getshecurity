Privacy

Updated 2026-10-25

This page covers getshecurity.com. For the app, read the app privacy policy.

Donations

Donations are handled by PayPal. We don't see or store your card or bank details. PayPal shares your name and email with us for the donation, which we use only to thank you and keep our books.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. Both may log your IP address as part of normal web hosting. We use no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and complain to your data protection authority.

In danger right now? Call your local emergency number first: 911 (US, CA), 112 (EU, NL), 999 (UK), 000 (AU).
